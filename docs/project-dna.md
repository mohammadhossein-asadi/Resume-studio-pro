## Purpose & Problem Statement

**Problem**: Job seekers need a modern resume builder with Persian support, multiple template options, and easy export capabilities for job applications.

**Solution**: A comprehensive resume builder web application with pre-designed templates, AI-assisted content, Persian localization, and one-click export to PDF and Word formats.

---

## Target Users

- **Primary**: Job seekers and career professionals in Persian-speaking regions
- **Secondary**: Career coaches and counseling services
- **Tertiary**: University career centers and recruitment agencies
